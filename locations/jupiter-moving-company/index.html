On the ground

Moving realities in Jupiter

Prestigious gated and golf communities

Communities like Admirals Cove and Jonathan's Landing run guard gates, ask for certificates of insurance, and set strict HOA moving windows. We handle the COI paperwork, coordinate gate access, and stick to the approved hours so nobody gets a complaint.

Acreage estates in Jupiter Farms

Out in Jupiter Farms the homes sit on big lots with long driveways and plenty of room between the road and the front door. We bring the right crew and equipment to handle the extra distance without dragging your furniture across the yard.

Waterfront and barrier-island access

Plenty of Jupiter homes sit along the Loxahatchee and Intracoastal, and Jupiter Island and Tequesta are just up the road. Tight waterfront lots and narrow island access take careful planning, and we map the approach before the truck rolls.

Summer heat, hurricane season, and seasonal residents

Jupiter summers run hot and storm season stretches June through November, so we plan early starts and book around the weather. We also help seasonal residents load in and out on the schedule that fits their time on the Treasure Coast.

Local questions

Jupiter movers, answered

Can you move me into a gated community like Admirals Cove or Jonathan's Landing?
Yes. We move into gated and golf communities all over Jupiter and we know the drill. Just let us know your gate procedure, HOA moving hours, and whether they need a certificate of insurance, and we will handle the check-in and timing so your move goes smooth.
How much does a move in Jupiter cost?
Every move is different, so we give a free estimate before you commit to anything. Cost depends on the size of your home, how much you are moving, and whether you need packing or specialty handling. Call us at 772-777-6364 and we will walk through it with you.
Should I book my move ahead of time?
Booking ahead is a good idea, especially during season and around the holidays when calendars fill up fast. Gated communities also limit their moving windows, so an early date gives us room to work around the rules. Reach out early and we will lock in a day that works.
Can you handle high-value and specialty pieces?
Yes. A lot of Jupiter homes have fine furniture, art, pianos, and other items that need extra care. Our crew wraps and pads everything and brings the right equipment for the tricky pieces. We are licensed and insured, so your belongings are covered.
Do you handle long distance moves out of Jupiter?
We are a licensed Florida intrastate mover, so we handle long distance moves anywhere within the state. Whether you are heading to Naples, Orlando, or the Panhandle, we can get you there. Long distance for us means long distance within Florida.
