On the ground

Moving realities in Stuart

Narrow downtown streets and older homes

Historic downtown Stuart has tight streets, older bungalows, and small driveways. We plan the truck placement ahead of time so we are not blocking traffic or scraping a low porch.

Waterfront condos and limited parking

Riverfront and waterfront condos around Stuart often have tight parking, loading zones, and shared elevators. We coordinate dock and elevator access so the move stays on schedule.

Gated and 55-plus communities

Many gated and 55-plus communities in Martin County have set moving windows and HOA rules. We work inside those hours and check in at the gate so there are no surprises.

Summer heat and hurricane season

Treasure Coast summers run hot and storm season is real. We pace the crew, hydrate, and keep an eye on the forecast when we book your date.

Local questions

Stuart movers, answered

Do you move within Stuart and around Martin County?
Yes. Most of our work is local moves around Stuart, Rio, Rocky Point, North River Shores, and the rest of Martin County. We handle houses, condos, and apartments, big or small. Call us at 772-777-6364 for a free estimate.
Can you move me long distance within Florida?
We do. Andersen is a licensed Florida intrastate mover, so we handle long distance moves anywhere inside the state. That covers a move from Stuart down to Miami, up to Jacksonville, or over to the Gulf Coast. We are not an interstate carrier, so all our long distance work stays in Florida.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. We are a licensed and insured Florida mover, registered as Fla. Mover Reg. No. 3030. We are family-owned and based right here on the Treasure Coast.
Do you work with HOA moving windows in gated communities?
We do this all the time in Stuart area communities. Tell us your community rules and the time window your HOA allows, and we will plan the crew and truck to fit it. Just give us the gate details ahead of the move.
