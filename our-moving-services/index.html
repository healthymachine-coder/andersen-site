What we do

Every kind of move

Seven services, one family crew. From a studio across town to an office across the state, we have the people, the truck and the plan to get you there.

01

Local Moving

Home and apartment moves across the Treasure Coast, handled by a family crew that shows up on time and treats your place like its own.

  • Furniture padded and shrink wrapped before it leaves the room
  • Disassembly and reassembly of beds, tables and basic furniture
  • Floor runners and door protection at both homes
See local moving →
02

Long Distance Moving

Heading from the Treasure Coast to Tampa, Orlando, Naples or the Panhandle? We pack it, pad it and deliver it with the same crew that loaded it.

  • A single dedicated crew from load to delivery
  • Full furniture padding and shrink wrap for the long haul
  • Tightly loaded and secured cargo so nothing shifts in transit
See long distance moving →
03

Packing & Unpacking

Full pack, partial pack or just the fragile stuff. We bring the materials, wrap it right and label every box by room so unpacking is painless.

  • Professional boxes, paper, bubble and tape
  • Dish packs and special boxes for fragile kitchenware
  • Wardrobe boxes so clothes move on the hanger
See packing & unpacking →
04

Residential Moving

Houses, condos, rentals and 55-plus communities. We handle the whole home with care, from the master bedroom to the last box in the garage.

  • A full walk through and plan before move day
  • Padding and shrink wrap on all furniture
  • Beds, tables and large pieces taken apart and rebuilt
See residential moving →
05

Commercial Moving

Offices, storefronts and professional suites relocated with a plan that keeps your downtime short and your gear accounted for.

  • A move plan built around your business hours
  • After hours and weekend scheduling to limit downtime
  • Desks, cubicles and conference furniture disassembled and rebuilt
See commercial moving →
06

Specialty Moving

Pianos, antiques, fine art, gun safes and heirlooms. The heavy, awkward and high-value items get extra hands, extra padding and a plan.

  • Upright and grand pianos
  • Antiques, heirlooms and delicate furniture
  • Fine art, mirrors and large framed pieces
See specialty moving →
07

Storage

Closing early, moving out before you move in, or staging a home for sale? We offer storage solutions to bridge the gap so your move does not depend on perfect timing.

  • A bridge when your move out and move in dates do not match
  • We load, transport and store your belongings
  • Furniture stays padded and wrapped while it is stored
