Down Three Floors and Into a House in Port St. Lucie

November 10, 2026 · 2 min read · Andersen Moving and Storage

Ryan and the Andersen Moving crew kept this one close to home: Port St. Lucie to Port St. Lucie, from a third floor apartment down to a first floor house.

Wrapped Before the Stairs

Before the couch went anywhere near the stairwell, the crew stood it on end, covered it in a moving pad and shrink wrapped it tight from top to bottom. That way the fabric, the frame and the building all make it down three floors without a mark.

Two Crews, One Day

Ryan ran this job himself while our second crew handled a move across the state. Two crews, two moves, same day.

Our Supervisor

And yes, that is an inflatable purple alien by the wall. He kept an eye on the whole thing. No complaints from him.

Why It Matters

Apartment stairwells are where furniture, railings and walls take the hits. Wrapping big pieces before they leave the room is how a third floor move ends with nothing to touch up at either address.
