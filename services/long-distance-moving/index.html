Long Distance Moving

A move across Florida is a different animal than a move across town. Your belongings spend hours on the road, the timing has to line up on both ends, and a dropped detail turns into a long, expensive headache. We handle long distance moves the way we handle everything: carefully, on time, and with no fine print.

Long distance means across Florida

Andersen Moving is a Florida company, registered with the state under Fla. Mover Reg. No. 3030. When we talk about long distance, we are talking about moves within Florida. From Stuart to Sarasota, Port St. Lucie to Pensacola, or Jupiter to Jacksonville, if it is inside the state, we can move you.

We are upfront about that because you deserve a mover who tells you exactly what they do and does it well, rather than promising the world.

Packed for the long haul

A piece of furniture that survives a ten minute drive can still get scuffed on a four hour one. For long distance moves we wrap heavier, load tighter and secure everything that goes on the truck. You get a clear delivery window and a direct line to the crew, so you always know where your home is.

Then the same crew that loaded your house unloads it, places it and puts the beds and tables back together. No handoffs, no mystery, no waiting around for a truck that may or may not show.

Included

What a long distance move includes

  • A single dedicated crew from load to delivery
  • Full furniture padding and shrink wrap for the long haul
  • Tightly loaded and secured cargo so nothing shifts in transit
  • Disassembly and reassembly of beds, tables and large furniture
  • Clear delivery window and direct contact along the way
  • Optional full packing so you do not lift a box

The process

How a long distance move works

  1. 01

    Plan and quote

One crew, one truck, one point of contact from your driveway to your new front door.

Good questions

Long Distance Moving questions

Do you move out of state?
Andersen Moving operates within Florida. When we say long distance, we mean long distance moves inside the state, from the Treasure Coast to anywhere in Florida. We are registered with the State of Florida under Fla. Mover Reg. No. 3030.
How is a long distance move priced?
Long distance moves usually factor in the distance, the size of your home and the amount of labor and materials. We give you a written estimate up front so there are no surprises when the truck arrives.
Will the same crew that loads my home deliver it?
Yes. We keep your move with one crew and one truck from start to finish, so the people who carefully packed your home are the ones who set it up at the other end.
