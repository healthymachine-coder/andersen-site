Packing & Unpacking

Packing is the part of moving everyone underestimates. It always takes longer than you think, and a box packed in a hurry is a box of broken things waiting to happen. Letting a crew that does this every day handle it is the single easiest way to take the stress out of a move.

Full pack, partial pack, your call

Some people want to hand us the keys and have the whole house boxed up. Others just want help with the kitchen, the china cabinet and the artwork. Either works. We will walk the home with you, point out what is worth packing professionally, and build a plan that fits how hands on you want to be.

Fragile items get real protection. Dishes go in dish packs, clothes ride in wardrobe boxes, and glass, mirrors and electronics get wrapped properly instead of wedged between towels.

Labeled so unpacking is easy

Every box we pack gets labeled by room and contents. That means on the other end, boxes land where they belong and you are not opening fifteen of them looking for the coffee maker. If you want, we will unpack it all, set things up and take the empty boxes with us so your new place is ready to live in, not buried in cardboard.

Included

What packing includes

  • Professional boxes, paper, bubble and tape
  • Dish packs and special boxes for fragile kitchenware
  • Wardrobe boxes so clothes move on the hanger
  • Careful wrapping of glass, art and electronics
  • Every box labeled by room and contents
  • Optional unpacking and debris removal at the new home

Good questions

Packing & Unpacking questions

Can you pack just the kitchen or the fragile items?
Absolutely. A partial pack is one of our most popular options. Many people handle the easy stuff like clothes and books, then have us pack the kitchen, glassware, art and electronics where the risk is highest.
Do you provide the boxes and materials?
Yes. When we pack for you, we bring professional grade boxes, paper, bubble wrap and tape, including dish packs and wardrobe boxes. You do not need to buy a thing.
Can you unpack too?
We can. Tell us where things go and we will unpack, place items and take the empty boxes and packing debris with us so you are not drowning in cardboard.
