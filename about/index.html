Our story

A family crew, not a franchise

Andersen Moving and Storage is a family-owned moving company based on Florida's Treasure Coast. When you call, you reach the people who actually load the truck.

Built on the boring stuff that matters

We started Andersen Moving for a simple reason. Too many moves go sideways because the movers show up late, treat the furniture like it is disposable, or hit you with a bill that looks nothing like the quote. We thought the Treasure Coast deserved better than that.

So we built a crew around the things that actually make a move good. We show up on time. We wrap your furniture before it leaves the room. We put down floor protection at both ends. And we tell you the price up front, then stick to it.

One crew, start to finish

Because we are family-owned, you are not handed off between strangers. The people who load your truck are the people who unload it. We keep our crews tight and our communication clear, so nothing gets lost in translation and nothing gets lost on the truck.

From a studio apartment in St. Lucie West to a four-bedroom house in Jupiter, from a single piano to a whole office, every job gets the same careful hands and the same honest quote.
