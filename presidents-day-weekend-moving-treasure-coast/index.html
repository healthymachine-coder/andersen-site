Why Presidents Day Weekend Is the Smartest Time to Move on the Treasure Coast

February 12, 2026 · 13 min read · Andersen Moving and Storage

Moving is stressful enough without trying to squeeze everything into a regular two-day weekend. Presidents Day weekend offers Treasure Coast residents a rare opportunity to use a three-day weekend for moving, giving you extra time to pack, settle in, and recover without burning vacation days. This guide shows you why mid-February is one of the best times to move locally within Port St. Lucie, Stuart, Jensen Beach, and surrounding areas.

TLDR: Presidents Day weekend gives you an extra day to move and unpack without using PTO. February offers better pricing and availability than summer peak season, plus comfortable mid-70s weather instead of 90-degree heat. Smart planning six to eight weeks ahead helps you lock in dates and avoid last-minute stress.

You’ve been thinking about moving for weeks. Maybe you found the perfect place in Port St. Lucie, or you’re downsizing to a condo in Stuart. Either way, you’re dreading the logistics: packing everything you own, coordinating with movers, and somehow getting it all done while working full-time.

Most people try to cram their entire move into a single Saturday. By Sunday night, they’re exhausted, surrounded by half-unpacked boxes, and facing a Monday morning commute to work feeling completely drained. There’s a better way.

Presidents Day weekend, which falls on the third Monday of February every year, gives you that crucial extra day. It transforms moving from a frantic sprint into a manageable process with built-in recovery time. Here’s exactly why this long weekend is your best choice for a Treasure Coast move.

Why a 3-Day Weekend Move Makes Life Easier

A three-day moving timeline changes everything about how you experience the process. Instead of rushing through every step, you can break the work into manageable chunks that don’t leave you feeling overwhelmed.

More Time Means Less Stress

When you have three full days instead of two, you can pace yourself properly. Saturday becomes your main moving day with professional movers handling the heavy lifting. Sunday gives you time to unpack essentials, set up your bedroom and kitchen, and get your internet working. Monday, the federal holiday, becomes your buffer day for finishing touches, running errands, and actually resting before returning to work Tuesday.

This schedule works especially well for working professionals and families with school-age children. You’re not sacrificing precious vacation days, yet you get the breathing room you need to handle unexpected challenges like delayed utility connections or furniture that needs assembly.

Presidents Day Weekend Plus February: Better Conditions Than Summer

Mid-February offers significant advantages over the traditional summer moving season. Both weather and market conditions work in your favor when you move during this timeframe.

Weather Makes a Bigger Difference Than You Think

Treasure Coast summers are brutal for moving. High temperatures regularly hit the upper 80s to low 90s with extreme humidity and frequent afternoon thunderstorms. Moving furniture in those conditions exhausts both you and your moving crew, and the heat can damage temperature-sensitive items like electronics, artwork, and wood furniture.

February offers a completely different experience. According to National Weather Service climate data, typical Treasure Coast temperatures in mid-February range from the low to mid-70s with much lower humidity than summer months. The weather is comfortable for physical work, and afternoon storms are rare. Your belongings stay safer, your moving crew stays energized, and you won’t spend the day drenched in sweat.

Off-Peak Pricing and Availability

Late spring and summer represent peak moving season when demand for moving services spikes dramatically. More people compete for fewer available time slots, which drives prices up and makes scheduling more difficult.

According to the American Moving & Storage Association, approximately 70% of moves occur between May and September, creating intense competition for moving services during those months. February falls squarely in the off-peak moving season. While Presidents Day weekend itself is a popular choice, the overall month sees much less competition than summer months. This often translates to more flexible scheduling options and potentially lower costs.

Success starts with proper planning, ideally six to eight weeks before the holiday weekend. Early booking ensures you get your preferred date and gives you time to organize everything properly.

Choosing a Registered Moving Company

When selecting your moving company, verify they’re properly registered with Florida’s regulatory authorities. The Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services oversees moving company registration in Florida. All legitimate moving companies must have a Florida Mover Registration number, which you can verify before booking.

Local Considerations for Treasure Coast Moves

Many Treasure Coast communities have specific rules about moving activities, especially on weekends and holidays. Gated communities in Port St. Lucie, Stuart, and Vero Beach often require advance notification, moving permits, or specific time windows. Some HOAs prohibit weekend moves entirely or restrict them to certain hours.

Contact your HOA or property management company early in the planning process to understand any restrictions. This prevents last-minute surprises on moving day.

Presidents Day Sales: Move and Upgrade Simultaneously

Presidents Day is famous for major sales on furniture, mattresses, and appliances. This creates a unique opportunity to replace old items instead of paying to move them.

Strategic Shopping During Your Move

Instead of hauling worn-out furniture to your new place, you can sell or donate it and buy replacements during the holiday sales. Schedule deliveries for late Sunday or Monday of your moving weekend, giving your moving crew time to complete their work first.

This approach works particularly well for:

  • Mattresses and box springs (Presidents Day mattress sales are legendary)
  • Large appliances if your new place needs them
  • Heavy furniture pieces that are expensive to move
  • Items that don’t fit your new space layout

Before shopping, measure doorways, hallways, and rooms in your new place carefully. Many Treasure Coast homes have different layouts and access points than what you’re used to, so verify that new furniture will actually fit before making purchases.

Treasure Coast Specifics for Holiday Weekend Moves

Local factors significantly impact moving logistics on the Treasure Coast, especially during a popular three-day weekend when more people are out and about.

Traffic and Timing Considerations

Beach communities like Jensen Beach and Jupiter may see increased traffic during Presidents Day weekend as residents and visitors enjoy the pleasant February weather. Starting your move early on Saturday morning, ideally by 8 AM, helps you avoid congestion and complete the majority of work before midday heat.

Some gated communities become busier during holiday weekends as snowbirds enjoy the area. Work with your moving company to plan arrival times that work around community patterns and gate access procedures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is Presidents Day weekend actually a good time to move on the Treasure Coast?

A: Yes, Presidents Day weekend is one of the smartest times to move locally on the Treasure Coast. You get an extra day without using vacation time, enjoy comfortable February weather instead of summer heat, and often benefit from off-peak pricing. The key is booking early, since this popular weekend fills up quickly with other movers who understand these same advantages.

Q: How far in advance should I book a Presidents Day weekend move?

A: Book six to eight weeks ahead to ensure availability. While February is generally an off-peak month, the three-day weekend itself is popular for moves precisely because it offers that extra day. Early booking gives you first choice of dates and time slots.

Q: Do moving companies work on Presidents Day Monday, or just Saturday and Sunday?

A: Most professional moving companies operate on Presidents Day Monday since it’s a federal holiday but not universally observed by private businesses. Confirm your moving company’s availability when booking. Some families prefer to use Monday as their main moving day to avoid weekend premium rates, though this varies by company.

Q: Is moving during a three-day weekend more expensive than a regular weekend?

A: Not necessarily. While the long weekend itself is more popular, February is an off-peak month overall, which often results in better rates than summer peak season. Some companies may charge weekend premiums regardless of whether it’s a long weekend, so ask about pricing when getting quotes.

Q: What’s the best way to use all three days of the long weekend?

A: Use Saturday for the main move with professional movers handling loading, transport, and unloading. Dedicate Sunday to unpacking essentials and setting up your bedroom, kitchen, and bathroom. Save Monday for finishing touches, furniture assembly, running errands for things you need, and rest before returning to work Tuesday.

Q: Will my belongings be safer moving in February weather than summer heat?

A: Yes, February’s mild temperatures and lower humidity are significantly better for your belongings. Summer heat can damage electronics, warp wood furniture, and cause problems with heat-sensitive items. February’s comfortable 70s temperatures reduce these risks considerably.

Q: Can I schedule packing services before Presidents Day weekend?

A: Absolutely. Many families schedule professional packing for Friday afternoon or evening before the long weekend begins. This approach maximizes your efficiency by having movers pack Friday, move Saturday, and giving you Sunday and Monday to unpack and settle in. Ask your moving company about packing service availability.

Q: What if my first-choice date on Presidents Day weekend is already booked?

A: Consider moving the weekend before or after Presidents Day. You’ll still enjoy February’s weather advantages and off-peak pricing, though you won’t have the extra holiday Monday. Alternatively, if you have vacation time available, taking Monday off after a regular weekend move gives you the same three-day benefit.

Q: Do I need special permits to move on a holiday weekend in Treasure Coast communities?

A: Requirements vary by community. Many HOAs and gated communities require moving permits or advance notification regardless of the day, while others have specific rules about weekend and holiday moves. Contact your HOA or property management at least four to six weeks ahead to understand any restrictions or requirements.
